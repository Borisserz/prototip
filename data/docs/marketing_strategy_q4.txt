=== MARKETING STRATEGY Q4 ===
Campaign: Black Friday / Cyber Monday E-commerce Push
Focus: Электроника (Electronics)

Overview:
Historical data shows that clothing (Одежда) dips heavily during the summer months (June-August). However, Q4 is our biggest opportunity for high-ticket items, specifically in the "Электроника" category.

Action Plan:
Starting November 20th, we will apply a blanket `discount_applied = 1` (30% off) across the entire electronics catalogue. 
Expect a massive 300% multiplier on sales volume in November and a 200% multiplier throughout December due to holiday shopping. 

Stock levels in Minsk and Warsaw distribution centers must be doubled by November 1st.
