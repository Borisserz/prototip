# Internal Memo: SaaS Pricing Update (Confidential)
**Date:** December 15, 2022
**From:** CEO
**To:** All Staff

Team,

As discussed in Q4 strategy meetings, we are facing increased server costs. Effective **January 1, 2023**, the price of our **Basic** plan will increase from $8/seat to $10/seat.
Pro and Enterprise tiers will remain unaffected for now.

**Risk Assessment:**
Our product team projects a temporary spike in the `churn_rate` (отток клиентов) among Basic tier subscribers during January and February 2023. We expect up to 15% churn in this segment. 

**Mitigation:**
Customer Success must focus heavily on retaining Enterprise clients during this period. We accept the churn in the lower tier as a necessary step towards profitability.
